WebCodecs hardware H.264 decode produces stale macroblocks

This page decodes one 240-frame H.264 stream twice in your browser — once with hardwareAcceleration: 'prefer-hardware', once with 'prefer-software' — and compares the pixels. The bytes are identical; only the decoder differs. Where the two disagree, the hardware decoder has silently left macroblocks at their previous contents. No error is raised and every chunk produces an output frame.
